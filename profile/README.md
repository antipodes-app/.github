<h3 align="center">Talk by voice to one stranger, somewhere else on Earth.</h3>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://antipodes.app"><b>antipodes.app</b></a>
</p>

---

**Antipodes** puts you on a voice call with one random person, somewhere else on the planet, around a pixel globe
that shows where they are. The name comes from the point on the exact opposite side of the Earth: somewhere, someone
is standing sole to sole with you.

- 🎙️ **Voice only.** No video, no ads, no sign-up.
- 🌍 **Somewhere else.** The globe draws the arc between you and the person you're talking to.
- 🛡️ **Moderated live.** Guardian listens on your own device and keeps nothing unless someone reports; every report is reviewed by a person.
- 👥 **Friends, if you click.** Add someone after a call, message them, call them back.

Adults only. Made in France.
